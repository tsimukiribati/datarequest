How it works
  1. You sign in with your email and answer a few short questions.
  2. You get a reference number straight away.
  3. The Ministry reviews your request for approval.
  4. An officer sends the data to your email.

We share summary figures only. We do not give out the names or personal details of students or teachers.

Sign in with your email

Use your own email, such as Gmail, Outlook or Yahoo. We send a code of 6 numbers to it. This shows us the request comes from a real person, and it is where we send your data.
